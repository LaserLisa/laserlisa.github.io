About Me

Hello!

I'm Lisa, a multidisciplinary robotics engineer with an MSc in Robotics from EPFL. I like to work close to the hardware: from mechanical design in CAD and custom PCBs to embedded firmware, control and experiments on the real robot. Whether it's a snake-like robot, an autonomous competition robot or a solar-hydrogen boat, I enjoy taking systems from the first sketch all the way to a working prototype.

Engineering Projects Showcase

Undulatory Snake-like Robot with CPG Control & Shear-Force Sensing - MSc Thesis at Tohoku University, Japan

About

Design, build and evaluation of a 10-segment planar undulatory robot with one passive degree of freedom per segment, driven by central pattern generators (CPGs) and equipped with local shear-force sensing.

Outcomes

A working robot with custom ESP32 header board and per-segment sensor boards, a matching MuJoCo simulation, and a set of experiments studying adaptive behaviour in undulatory locomotion.

Acquired Skills

Fusion360, KiCad PCB design, ESP-IDF, CPG-based control, MuJoCo, Python, experiment design

Autonomous DUPLO-Cleaning Robots - Winner of the 2025 EPFL Robot Competition

About

Z-Bot: Zebrafish-like Swimming Robot - Semester project & Science Robotics paper, BIOROB EPFL

About

Implemented closed-loop depth control in a zebrafish-like robot as a semester project, then characterized its Dynamixel actuators as a research assistant for a study on the energy efficiency of intermittent swimming.

Outcomes

Integrated depth sensing and control in the robot, built an experimental setup to measure motor efficiency and payload, and co-authored a Science Robotics paper.

Acquired Skills

Sensor selection & integration, closed-loop control, Dynamixel SDK, test-bench design, Python data analysis
