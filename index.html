About Me

Hello!

I'm Lisa, a multidisciplinary robotics engineer with an MSc in Robotics from EPFL. I like to work close to the hardware: from custom PCBs, embedded firmware, and mechanical design in CAD to control and experiments on the real robot. I care about reliable, thought-through designs and haven't lost the sparkling in my eyes when metal awakes to life.

Engineering Projects Showcase

Undulatory Snake-like Robot - MSc Thesis in the Ishiguro laboratory at Tohoku University, Japan und Prof. Auke Ijspeert

About

Design, build and evaluation of a 10-segment planar undulatory robot with one passive degree of freedom per segment, driven by central pattern generators (CPGs) and equipped with local shear-force sensing.

Outcomes

A robot with a custom ESP32 header board and sensor boards, a MuJoCo simulation, and a set of experiments studying adaptive behaviour in undulatory locomotion.

Acquired Skills

Fusion360, KiCad, ESP32 / ESP-IDF, C, CPG control, MuJoCo, Python, experiment design

Zebrafish-inspired Swimming Robot - Semester project in BIOROB EPFL under Prof. Auke Ijspeert

About

Implemented closed-loop depth control in a zebrafish-like robot as a semester project, then characterized its Dynamixel actuators as a research assistant for a study on the energy efficiency of intermittent swimming.

Outcomes

Integrated depth sensing and control in the robot, built an experimental setup to measure motor efficiency and payload, and co-authored a Science Robotics paper.

Acquired Skills

Altium Designer, sensor selection & integration, closed-loop control, C++, Python, Motor characterization
