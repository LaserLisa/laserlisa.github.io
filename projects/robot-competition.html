EPFL Robot Competition 2025

Team project · EPFL, Lausanne · Winner 2025

Type
Robot competition, team of three
Where
EPFL, Lausanne
When
2025
Result
1st place

Overview

The task: autonomously clean a dynamic arena of DUPLO assemblies. In a team of three we mechanically and electrically designed and implemented two autonomous differential-drive robots, and won the 2025 edition of the EPFL Robot Competition.

What I did

  • Mechanical & electrical design of two differential-drive robots.
  • Localization with LIDAR and AMCL.
  • Navigation with the MPPI controller from the Nav2 stack in ROS 2.
  • Perception: a YOLO model detecting and locating DUPLOs with the on-board camera.

Results

Winner of the 2025 EPFL Robot Competition.
