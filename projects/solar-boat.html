Overview

Swiss Solar Boat is an EPFL MAKE project building a hydrogen-solar powered foiling boat. As a member of the electronics team I worked on bringing the boat's electrical system together: integrating components mechanically and electrically, and designing interface electronics.

What I did

  • Mechanical and electrical integration of diverse electrical components in the boat.
  • PCB design in Altium for CAN communication and interfacing analog sensors.

Tools & Skills

  • Altium Designer
  • CAN bus
  • Analog sensors
  • System integration
  • Soldering
