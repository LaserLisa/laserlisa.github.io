Overview

Swiss Solar Boat is an EPFL MAKE project building a hydrogen-solar powered foiling boat. I was member of the electronics team and worked on integrating the boat's electrical system. This included integrating components mechanically and electrically, and designing interface electronics.

What I did

  • PCB design in Altium for CAN communication and interfacing analog sensors.
  • Cable selection and management in the boat.
  • Custom busbar specifications definition for power distribution and manufacturing.

Tools & Skills

  • Altium Designer
  • System integration
  • Soldering
  • Protocols: CAN & EtherCAT
  • Metal work: milling & drilling
